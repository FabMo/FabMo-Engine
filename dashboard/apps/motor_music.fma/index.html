Motor Music

— —
Playing moves the machine. Each note shuttles an axis back and forth inside a small area around where the tool is now. Spindle off, tool raised, stay clear.
Play with the computer keyboard: bottom letter row (Z…/) is the lower octave with S D G H J as its black keys; Q…P with the number row is the octave above. ↑ ↓ shift octaves · hold Shift for vibrato · Space silences · Esc stops playing. The picture of the keyboard is for display only.
