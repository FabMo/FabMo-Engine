2 Find the marks
No camera or machine needed: a virtual sheet is laid crooked and slightly stretched on a virtual table, and a virtual camera looks for the dots.
Load a job first.
Jog until mark 1 is in the camera view, at the height you calibrated the camera at, then press Find marks. The machine will move to the rest.
Camera settings
Scale and rotation: put any dot in view, then press the button. The machine moves 0.25 in in X and Y and watches the dot shift.
Camera offset: drill a small hole, and with the bit still over it press “Bit is over the hole”. Jog until the camera sees the hole, then press “Camera sees the hole”.
